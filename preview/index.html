Questions parents ask

Is this a membership? Will I be charged again?
No. The $27 is a one-time charge for 2 classes and a free Impact shirt. Nothing renews. After the trial, we explain membership options and you decide.
Can I get a refund?
The trial fee is non-refundable. If you need a different class time, contact us at least 24 hours before the class at 954 636 1515 or info@imaimpact.com and we will reschedule.
My kid is shy or lacks confidence. Can this help?
Absolutely! Our program helps kids build confidence one class at a time. Through fun challenges, encouragement, and small wins, kids learn to believe in themselves.
My kid isn't very athletic or has never trained. Will they fit in?
Of course! No experience needed. Our coaches guide kids step by step at their own pace. Everyone starts somewhere!
Is Impact traditional karate?
Impact is more than traditional martial arts. Our martial arts and athletic performance training combines martial arts, boxing, self-defense, and athletic training to build strength, speed, coordination, confidence, and discipline.
